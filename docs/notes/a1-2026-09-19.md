## The change

`app/hooks/useRouting.ts`, the `MULTI_LABELS`/`STRENGTHS` per-segment branch:

- `updateProgress("Calculating route legs", …)` now fires **once per strength
  pass** (3 updates) instead of once per leg (12), reporting `si + 1` of
  `STRENGTHS.length`.
- The per-leg `await yieldToBrowser()` calls are gone. The previous A1 draft
  kept one yield per pass, but each yield handed the page a full render/paint,
  and on this SwiftShader box three re-paints still cost ~1.5 s of the span. The
  three passes are sub-millisecond searches — there is nothing to freeze
  between — so the loop now runs unpainted and takes **one** yield after the
  pass-batched search to flush progress and the `si === 0` preview.
- `updatePreview` is untouched (still per leg, still `si === 0` only).
- Abort/supersede checks, `connectRouteEndpoints`, legs, provenance,
  `recordRoutingRun`, and every search constant are untouched; routing output is
  byte-identical (`app/lib/__tests__/routing.test.ts` and the e2e route smoke
  both pass).
