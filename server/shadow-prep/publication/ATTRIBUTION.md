# Generation attribution template

Each local generation writes `ATTRIBUTION.json` from the admitted source manifest.
It must retain the DCP boundary notice, FABDEM CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 and modification
notice with terrain-family artifacts, Overture/OSM ODbL notices and any required
derivative-database access, CHMv2 CC BY 4.0 attribution (Meta, WRI and Vantor
imagery notice), and installed-grid notices. Building and canopy objects never
receive terrain-derived absolute values or FABDEM terms merely through composition.
# Source use policy

NYC source preparation in this project is for personal, non-commercial use only.
FABDEM V1.2-derived output retains FABDEM attribution and is subject to CC BY-NC-SA
4.0. FABDEM-derived terrain may be shared for noncommercial purposes under CC BY-NC-SA
4.0, with required attribution, modification notices, and ShareAlike terms. Commercial use
(including ads, paid access, or distribution to customers) requires separate permission
from the FABDEM rights holders. (License interpretation, not formal legal advice.)
